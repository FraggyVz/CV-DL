### Gin's comments

**Round 7:** Keep `A_SPLIT`. The palace player does not have to make it onto the
site. Being stopped or killed inside palace still counts when the team is
attempting a split onto A.

**Round 12:** Leave this pending. Nobody attacks through ramp or palace, so it is
not `A_SPLIT`. It is a mid-to-A attack, which we do not currently have a class for.

**Round 17:** Leave this as it is, without an attack segment.

**Round 21:** I would label this `B_DIRECT`. The flanker did not arrive on site
with the apartments group, so I would treat him as a lurker.
